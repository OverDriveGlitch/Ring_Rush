Explain what Ring Rush is and how the water ring toss game works.

Ring Rush is a digital recreation of the classic hand held water ring toss toy that many people grew up playing. 

We were inspired by the hackathon theme "revitalize", our project brings back a forgotten childhood toy into a digitalized interactive game. 

How does it work? 
- Player controls two water jets located at the bottom of the tank
- Pressing a jet pushes the water up that pushes the rings
- Rings move through the water using our water physics
- Players must carefully position the rings onto the pegs
- The objective is to land as many rings as possible

RingRush recreates this using Python and Pygame
